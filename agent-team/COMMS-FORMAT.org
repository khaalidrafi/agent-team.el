* WHY THIS EXISTS
  A team message to the Freshorama team (flagged by the Owner in #hq on
  2026-10-26) was written in an informal, non-standard shape. The Owner's
  ruling: team communication is a professional surface. A letter carries
  the whole team's competence in its formatting — a sloppy letter reads
  as sloppy engineering, and a client cannot be asked to decode our
  internal shorthand. This file is the single standard; no team, seat, or
  lead invents a private format beside it.

* SCOPE — WHAT THIS GOVERNS
  A LETTER is any written message whose sender is a TEAM and whose
  recipient is a party outside the seat-to-seat work chat:
  - team -> team
    - admincraft <-> docneural <-> agent-team.el,
    - any future team,
  - team -> client
    - e.g. Freshorama e-commerce,
  - team -> Owner
    - formal requests, offers, and reports of record,
    - quick #hq pings stay conversational; the Owner is exempt, teams
      are not.
  Internal TEAM-CHAT.org dispatch/report/ask traffic keeps the existing
  headline + drawer FORMAT LAW untouched. An entry that is ALSO a letter
  satisfies BOTH grammars:
  - the local headline + drawer grammar,
  - :KIND: letter (extends the KIND enum by Owner order),
  - the extra drawer fields below,
  - the letter body skeleton.

* WHERE A LETTER LIVES
  - Default: a TEAM-CHAT.org entry
    - keep the local MSG headline grammar exactly,
    - set :KIND: letter,
    - add the drawer fields,
    - write the body in the skeleton below.
  - A client deliverable or standalone letter file
    - one Org headline per letter, tagged :letter:<from>:<to>:,
    - same drawer and body.
  - Relayed through chat/email
    - relayed VERBATIM — the format travels with the text,
    - never paraphrase a letter into slang on the way out.

* THE LETTER SKELETON (every field mandatory, every time)
#+BEGIN_EXAMPLE
,* MSG <from> -> @<to> :letter:                    :msg:<from>:<to>:letter:
:PROPERTIES:
:FROM: <team-name> (signed by <seat/agent>)
:TO: <team-name | client-name>
:KIND: letter
:WHEN: [YYYY-MM-DD Eee HH:MM UTC]    (Org inactive timestamp)
:SUBJECT: <one line, <= 80 chars, no trailing period>
:REF: <headline/date of the letter this answers, or "new thread">
:PRIORITY: normal | high | urgent    (urgent = an Owner-visible delay)
:REPLY-BY: <YYYY-MM-DD | none>
:STATUS: open | answered | closed
:END:
  To the <recipient team name>,

  <PURPOSE — one short paragraph, 1-3 sentences: why you write and what
  you ask for. No warm-up, no filler.>

  <BODY — the substance, structured with nested Org lists where more
  than one fact travels together. Every claim is anchored to an
  artifact: path:line, commit sha, command + pasted output, or URL.
  Measured numbers beat adjectives.>

  <ACTION REQUESTED — numbered list. Each item = one ask, then nested
  sub-lines carry the contract:>
  1. <the ask, one sentence>
     - owner: <role or team who executes>,
     - by: <YYYY-MM-DD | "no deadline">,
     - evidence expected: <what a correct fulfilment looks like>.
  <If the letter asks nothing, write exactly: "No action requested —
  this letter is for the record.">

  Regards,
  <team-name> — <signing seat/agent>
#+END_EXAMPLE
  Standalone letter files replace the MSG headline with:
  =* LETTER <subject> — <from> -> @<to> :letter:   :letter:<from>:<to>:=

* THE MUSTS (eleven)
  1. Every drawer field is present, every time
     - including one-line letters.
  2. Salutation opens, signature closes
     - an unsigned message is not a letter; it is a stray note.
  3. PURPOSE lands inside the first three sentences.
  4. ONE subject per letter
     - a second subject is a second letter with its own :REF:.
  5. Every claim carries an artifact anchor
     - "obviously" is not an anchor.
  6. Every ask is an explicit, numbered action
     - with the nested contract: owner, by-date, evidence expected,
     - "please look into this" is forbidden — say what, who, by when,
     - if there is truly no deadline, the item says "no deadline".
  7. PROFESSIONAL REGISTER
     - complete sentences, no slang, no emoji, no chat abbreviations,
     - no ALL-CAPS shouting, no sarcasm, no blame,
     - neutral and courteous: address the team, not the person.
  8. APPEND-ONLY HISTORY
     - a correction is a NEW letter whose :REF: names the letter it
       corrects,
     - never edit or delete a sent letter.
  9. STATUS LIFECYCLE
     - the sender marks :STATUS: answered or closed in the NEXT letter
       it sends on that :REF:,
     - never by rewriting history.
  10. CHANNEL-AGNOSTIC
     - the format travels with the text: TEAM-CHAT.org entry, letter
       file, chat relay — all identical.
  11. STRUCTURE (Owner order 2026-10-26: nested lists)
     - top level = one idea per bullet,
     - sub-details nested underneath their parent,
     - never a prose wall, never a flat ten-line list.

* MUST-NOTS (reviewers reject on sight)
  - Format violations
    - unsigned, salutation-less, or bottom-buried-ask letters,
    - mixed-subject letters,
    - deadline-free asks (say "no deadline" explicitly if truly none).
  - Register violations
    - internal slang, seat names, or board codes in a client-facing
      letter — the client does not know our jargon; expand it or drop
      it,
    - emoji, stickers, or exclamation-mark enthusiasm in client letters.
  - Substance violations
    - promises of dates, numbers, or deliverables not already anchored
      in the repo.

* ENFORCEMENT
  - TRIGGER CASE
    - the Owner ordered this standard in #hq on 2026-10-26 after a team
      message to Freshorama went out non-formal,
    - FIRST APPLICATION: the next message to Freshorama is re-issued in
      this format before it leaves the team.
  - Reviewers and leads reject a non-conforming letter the same way
    they reject a red test suite; the reject reason cites this file.
  - Precedent
    - a conforming letter that passed review becomes the precedent,
    - when in doubt, copy the most recent :STATUS: closed letter.
  - This file is Lead-owned; changes require the Owner's sign-off.
