#+STARTUP: overview

* What a workflow is

A team's workflow is its dispatch vocabulary, its role skeleton, and
its review gate. The board carries it as a =#+WORKFLOW:= line
(=agent-team-set-workflow=; =agent-team-init= with a prefix seeds it).
Coding is the default and stays exactly as it was — a workflow line is
optional and nothing existing reads differently without one.

| workflow   | dispatch types                 | role skeleton                                      | review gate                                              |
|------------+--------------------------------+----------------------------------------------------+----------------------------------------------------------|
| coding     | fix, feature, refactor, test, release | Software Engineer, QA Engineer, Release Engineer, Platform Engineer | a second seat code-reviews before REVIEW flips     |
| writing    | draft, edit, fact-check, publish | Researcher, Drafter, Editor, Fact-Checker          | an adversarial read by a second seat; every claim carries its source |
| reporting  | spike, draft, verify, file     | Researcher, Drafter, Verifier, Editor              | every non-witnessed claim attributed; anything unverified reads OPEN |
| research   | investigate, synthesize, review | Investigigator, Synthesizer, Skeptic               | the Skeptic argues the opposite before the report lands |

* Laws that hold in every workflow

- The board stays the single source of truth; a workflow changes what
  lands on it, never where truth lives.
- The keyword cycle, TIME DISCIPLINE LAW, and CONSULT LAW are
  workflow-independent.
- Seats are still model@platform pairs from the umbrella CANDIDATES
  (MEMBERS.org); the workflow names the ROLES a seat dresses in, not a
  second hiring pipeline.
- The dispatch composer appends a =WORKFLOW:= line naming the gate on
  every non-coding dispatch, so the receiving seat sees the vocabulary
  without reading this file.

* Why these four

- coding: the original team shape; unchanged.
- writing: blogs and long-form — multi-pass structure (research,
  draft, edit, verify) is what makes prose trustworthy.
- reporting: news media — the verifier role and the OPEN marker keep
  attribution honest under deadline.
- research: investigations — the skeptic role is the adversarial pass
  the interrogate playbook pays for, run as a seat.

A new workflow is a defvar entry in =agent-team.el= plus a row here;
roles without a seat stay unfilled until the umbrella staffing cycle
fills them (ADD-FREEZE law holds).
